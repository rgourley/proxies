# Proxies

Every markdown file in this repo uses clear, professional business English. That includes the README, the skill, the formats, the seats, the orgs, and the proxies. The rules are in the "Plain business English" section of `.claude/skills/proxies/SKILL.md`.

`ONTOLOGY.md` defines every term in this repo: org, proxy, seat, knowledge, artifact, format, run, round, response, summary, and the rest. Use those terms in files, code, and chat. Do not use synonyms. Add a new term to `ONTOLOGY.md` before you use it.
