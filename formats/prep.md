# Format: prep

A pre-read for the real meeting. One blind round, in parallel. No rebuttal, no decision.

Each proxy writes:
- **First reaction**: one sentence, in voice.
- **Top three questions** they will ask in the real meeting, in the order they will ask them. Each question is in the proxy's voice and names the step, node, or screen it is about.
- **The answer that satisfies it**: for each question, what a presenter must say or show.

Aim for about 300 words.

## Verdict
Every response starts with one line after the title: `**Verdict**: <Block | Rework | Ship with changes | Ship>. <one sentence on why>.` Block means you would stop it from your seat. Rework means it must change before you would use or approve it.

## Language
Plain business English. Name each step by what it does, in words. Never use diagram node letters or IDs. Explain acronyms the first time.
