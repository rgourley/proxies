## Who stops, and where

Three of five would not finish sign-up. The other two would finish but could not use the account as it is set up.

- **Rosa** stops at the first screen. A required phone number is where she closes the tab.
- **Walt** stops at the account form on his iPad. The labels disappear, the error does not say what he did wrong, and once he gets in he cannot find where to make an invoice.
- **Tomasz** stops after his first mistake. The error does not say what to fix, it clears his password, and he still does not know the password rules.
- **Marcus** finishes, but the form never says whose account he is creating for his boss, and one checkbox signs the owner up for marketing emails.
- **Priyanka** finishes, but the flow assumes one person runs one company. She has nine clients and nowhere to add a second business.

## What to fix first

1. **Keep what the person typed when the form has an error, say beside each field what to fix, and show the password rules before they type.** (Tomasz, Walt, Rosa)
2. **Cut sign-up to email and password, or a social sign-in with its name in words.** Drop the phone number, and ask for company size and the rest later, with a way to skip. (Rosa, Priyanka, Marcus, Walt)
3. **Make "Check your email" recoverable:** show the address, let the person change it, add a resend, and give the link longer than 15 minutes. (All 5)
4. **Put a visible "Create your first invoice" button on the empty dashboard.** (Walt, Rosa, Priyanka, Tomasz)
5. **Split the terms checkbox from marketing emails, and say whose account is being created.** (Marcus)
