# Priyanka, Freelance bookkeeper for nine small businesses

**Verdict**: Rework. The whole flow assumes one person runs one company, and there is no step where I can add a second business, so I cannot use it for the nine clients I came here to invoice for.

## 1. "Company name" is required, and there is only one box

**Where**: The create-your-account screen, the company name and company size fields.

**How it breaks**: I type my own name because I run a sole practice. Now my account is "Priyanka Bookkeeping". My first invoice today is for Harbor Street Bakery, and their name and address have to be on it. Nothing in the flow lets me add the bakery as a separate business. If I type the bakery's name here instead, my other eight clients have nowhere to go. In my accounting software I log in once and pick a client from a list. Here I would need nine accounts and nine email addresses. That breaks my first hard line.

**Severity**: Critical.

**Fix**: Add one question: "Who are you setting this up for? My own business / Clients I work for." For the client option, skip company name at sign-up and send me to "Add your first client business" before the dashboard.

## 2. Company size, phone number, and "how did you hear about us" are all required

**Where**: The create-your-account screen.

**How it breaks**: Company size for whom? I am one person, the bakery has 6 staff, and the landscaping firm has 40. None of those answers is true for the account. The phone number is my personal mobile, and I don't hand that out to a vendor before I've seen the product. "How did you hear about us" is a marketing question, not something I need to send an invoice. Three required fields that don't fit my situation is my second hard line. This is where I would close the tab.

**Severity**: High.

**Fix**: Make phone and "how did you hear about us" optional, or remove them. Drop company size from sign-up. Ask for it per business when I add each business.

## 3. "Tell us about your team" cannot be skipped and asks for team size again

**Where**: The personalize screen after I click the email link.

**How it breaks**: It asks for team size a second time, and industry. My clients are a bakery, a dental practice, and a roofer, so one industry is wrong for eight of the nine. Then it requires a written answer to "What's your biggest invoicing challenge?" before I can reach the product. My honest answer is "this sign-up form," but I still have to type something. There is no skip button. Every minute here is unbilled time for me.

**Severity**: High.

**Fix**: Add a "Skip for now" button. Remove the repeated team size question. Make the text area optional. Let me set industry per business, not per account.

## 4. The link expires in 15 minutes, with no resend and no email shown

**Where**: The check-your-email screen.

**How it breaks**: I signed up with a work address, and I use a few of them. The screen does not say which one the link went to. Then a client calls. I come back 20 minutes later, the link is dead, and there is no resend button. If I try to sign up again, I expect the email is already taken. Now I'm locked out of an account I never got into, and there is no name or address to ask for help.

**Severity**: High.

**Fix**: Show the address the link went to, with a "Change" link. Add a resend button. Make the link last at least 24 hours.

## 5. The dashboard has no client switcher and no visible way to make an invoice

**Where**: The dashboard, my first screen after sign-up.

**How it breaks**: I land on an empty table. "New invoice" is hidden in the overflow menu at the top right, so I have to hunt for it. The bigger problem is that nothing on the screen shows which business I am working in. With nine clients, the real risk is sending the dentist's invoice under the bakery's name. My clients pay me to keep their records apart. A tool that doesn't show me which books are open is a liability.

**Severity**: Medium.

**Fix**: Put a "Create your first invoice" button in the empty table. Show the current business name at the top of every screen, with a switcher once there is more than one.

## Also noted, not counted

The form errors clear both password fields and don't say which field is wrong. The password rules only appear after the first failed submit. The terms checkbox also signs me up for marketing emails. The Google, Microsoft, and Apple sign-in icons have no labels. Each of these is a small cost on its own. Together they make me fill in this long form at least twice.
