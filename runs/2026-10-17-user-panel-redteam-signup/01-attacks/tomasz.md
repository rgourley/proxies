# Tomasz, Owner of a small cleaning company

**Verdict**: Block. When I make a mistake on the sign-up form, the error does not tell me what to fix, it deletes my password, and I would give up before I send my first invoice.

I sign up on my phone, in the van, between two cleaning jobs. I want to send an invoice to a client today. English is my second language. I read it well, but I must not have to guess.

## 1. The error message does not say what is wrong

- **Where**: The create-account screen, after I press "Submit" and something is wrong.
- **How it breaks**: I fill in nine fields. I press "Submit". A red banner says "There was a problem with your submission." Two fields have a red border, but nothing says why. My phone number has spaces, maybe that is the problem? My email is my company Gmail, maybe it is not a "work email"? I do not know. I will not call anyone for help. I will try different things, and after three tries I will close the page.
- **Severity**: Critical. This breaks my first rule. An error must tell me plainly what to fix.
- **Fix**: Put a short message under each wrong field. For example: "Phone number: use numbers only." Keep the banner, but make it say how many fields need fixing.

## 2. A mistake makes me type my password again, and I still do not know the rules

- **Where**: The create-account screen, the password fields.
- **How it breaks**: I type a password I can remember, with 10 letters. I type it again. I press "Submit". The form deletes both passwords. Only now do I see the rules: 12 characters, a number, a symbol, an uppercase letter. On a phone keyboard, a symbol and an uppercase letter take extra taps. I type it twice again. If any other field is also wrong, the passwords are deleted again. I have to start over each time, and that breaks my second rule.
- **Severity**: Critical. I cannot recover from a mistake without starting over.
- **Fix**: Show the password rules before I type, under the field. Check each rule as I type. Do not delete the passwords when another field is wrong. Remove "Confirm password" and add a "show password" button.

## 3. The labels disappear, so I forget what each field is

- **Where**: The create-account screen, all fields.
- **How it breaks**: The name of each field is grey text inside the box. When I start to type, it is gone. On my phone, the keyboard covers half the screen. I go back to fix a red field and I see only "Kowalski Cleaning" or "7". Is that "Company name" or "Company size"? Then the social sign-in buttons are only pictures. I do not know the Microsoft or Apple picture well, and I would not tap a button I cannot read.
- **Severity**: High. I have to guess, and guessing on a form is how I make mistakes.
- **Fix**: Put the label above each field, always visible. Add words to the social buttons: "Continue with Google", and the same for the others.

## 4. The email screen does not show my email, and the link dies fast

- **Where**: The "Check your email" screen.
- **How it breaks**: The screen says "Almost there! We sent a link to your email." Which email? If I typed it wrong, I cannot see it and I cannot change it. Nothing arrives. There is no "send again" button. Or the email comes, but I am already driving to the next client. I open it 40 minutes later and the link is dead after 15 minutes. What do I do now? I think I must sign up again, and maybe the system says my email is already used.
- **Severity**: High. I lose everything I typed, and there is no clear way back.
- **Fix**: Show my email address on the screen, with "Change email" and "Send the link again". Keep the link working for at least 24 hours. If the link is old, give me a page with one button that sends a new link.

## 5. More questions, and then an empty page with no "New invoice" button

- **Where**: The "Tell us about your team" screen and the first dashboard.
- **How it breaks**: After the email, I must answer more questions. Team size again, I already said 7. I must write my "biggest invoicing challenge" before I can continue. I do not want to write an essay in English on my phone. I write "none" only to pass. Then I see an empty table with no message and no button. I want to make an invoice. I do not know that "New invoice" is hidden in the menu with three dots. I would think the app is not ready.
- **Severity**: Medium. I would probably find the menu in the end, but I would feel foolish and lose time I wanted for my client.
- **Fix**: Make the team questions optional, with a "Skip" button, and do not ask team size twice. On the empty dashboard, show one sentence, "You have no invoices yet", and a large "Create your first invoice" button.

## Words I had to guess at

- "Submit". Submit what? "Create account" is clearer.
- "There was a problem with your submission." Which problem?
- "Almost there!" Almost where? Say "One more step: open the email we sent to tomasz@...".
- "Let's personalize Ledgerly." I am not sure what this means for me. Say "A few questions about your company (you can skip)".
- The checkbox joins the terms and the marketing emails. I must say yes to advertising to make an account. I would not see this in small text on my phone, and I would not like it later.
