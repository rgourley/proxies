# Walt, Owner of a family plumbing business

**Verdict**: Block. I would not get past the account form on my iPad today, and once I did, I could not find where to make an invoice.

## 1. The account form loses its labels and then won't say what I did wrong

**Where**: The create-account screen and the error state after I press Submit.

**How it breaks**: I have large text on. The grey words inside the boxes are cut off, so I'm not sure which box is "Company size" and which is "Phone." I start typing my email and the word in the box is gone. Now I can't check what that box was for. I press Submit. A red banner says "There was a problem with your submission." Three boxes turn red, but nothing tells me why. Both password boxes are empty again. The password rules only show up now, and I never saw them before. I'd figure I made a mess of it, close the iPad, and call my daughter. That is the exact thing I'm trying not to do.

**Severity**: Critical.

**Fix**: Put a label above each box that stays put. Show the password rules before I type. When something is wrong, put a plain sentence under that box saying what is wrong, and keep what I typed, passwords included.

## 2. The invoice button is hidden behind an icon

**Where**: The dashboard, the first screen after sign-up.

**How it breaks**: I finally get in. I see an empty table with "Invoice, Client, Amount, Due, Status" across the top and nothing else. No button says "New invoice." The only way to make one is in a menu in the top right corner. That menu is just an icon, probably three dots. I don't tap things when I don't know what they do. I came here to bill the Hendersons for a water heater today. I would sit there looking at an empty table and think the account hadn't finished setting up.

**Severity**: Critical.

**Fix**: Put a big button that says "Create your first invoice" in the middle of the empty table. Keep the menu if you want, but don't make it the only way.

## 3. The email link runs out in 15 minutes, and I can't get another one

**Where**: The "Almost there!" screen that tells me to check my email.

**How it breaks**: The screen says "We sent a link to your email." It doesn't say which address. If I typed "wlat" instead of "walt," I would never know. I switch over to my mail, wait, look in a few folders, and maybe a customer calls. Twenty minutes later I find the email and tap the link, and it has expired. The screen has no resend button. I'd assume I broke something and have to start over, if I bother at all.

**Severity**: High.

**Fix**: Show the email address you sent to, with a way to fix it. Add a "Send it again" button. Let the link last at least a day.

## 4. The Google, Microsoft, and Apple buttons are pictures with no words

**Where**: The bottom of the create-account screen.

**How it breaks**: There are three small pictures under the Submit button and no words next to them. I have an Apple iPad and that would be the easy way for me. But I won't tap a picture when I'm guessing what it does. I don't want to send my details somewhere I didn't mean to. So I skip them and go back to the long form, which is the part that trips me up.

**Severity**: High.

**Fix**: Write the words on each button: "Sign up with Apple," "Sign up with Google," "Sign up with Microsoft." Put them above the long form, not below it.

## 5. Too many questions that have nothing to do with sending an invoice

**Where**: The create-account screen and the "Let's personalize Ledgerly" screen.

**How it breaks**: Before I can send one bill, I have to give my phone number, pick how I heard about you, pick a company size, and then pick the team size again on the next screen. Then a big box asks for my "biggest invoicing challenge," and I can't skip it. I'm a slow typist on glass. I'd stare at that box, not knowing what you want me to say. The agreement box also signs me up for marketing emails, and I can't say no to those without saying no to the whole thing.

**Severity**: Medium.

**Fix**: Ask only for name, email, and password to start. Make the team questions skippable, or ask them later. Give the marketing emails their own box, left unchecked.
