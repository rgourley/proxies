# June, First-time user with no context

**Verdict**: Block. I would not get past the account form on my phone in five minutes, and if I did, the email step would lose me for good.

## 1. The account form wipes my password and doesn't tell me why

**Where**: The account form, when I tap "Submit" and something is wrong.

**How it breaks**: I type a password I use elsewhere, like "Sunny2024". I tap Submit. A red banner says "There was a problem with your submission." Both password boxes are now empty. A few boxes have red borders, but nothing says what is wrong. Only now do I see the password needs 12 characters, a symbol, and a capital letter. The labels vanished when I typed, so I can't tell which red box is which. That is my second confusing thing. I close the tab and figure I did something wrong.

**Severity**: Critical. This is where I quit.

**Fix**: Show the password rules before I type. Keep what I typed after an error. Put a short message under each field that says what to change.

## 2. The "Check your email" screen is a dead end

**Where**: The "Almost there!" screen after the account form.

**How it breaks**: It says "We sent a link to your email." It doesn't say which email. On my phone I might have typed "june@gmial.com". I switch to my mail app. Nothing there. Maybe it is in spam, maybe I mistyped. There is no resend button and no way to fix the address. Then a meeting starts. When I come back 20 minutes later the link has expired. I have no path forward, so I'm done.

**Severity**: Critical.

**Fix**: Show the address I typed with a "Change" link. Add a "Resend" button. Make the link last at least 24 hours.

## 3. Too many boxes before I've seen anything

**Where**: The account form, and then the "Let's personalize Ledgerly" screen.

**How it breaks**: On my phone the account form is nine fields long, and I have to scroll. Phone number, company size, and "How did you hear about us?" are all required. I don't know why an invoice app needs my phone number yet. Then after the email step, it asks my team size again, and wants me to write out my "biggest invoicing challenge" with no skip. I have five minutes. I'd stop at the essay box and think, "I'll do this later." I won't.

**Severity**: High.

**Fix**: Ask only for email and password up front. Drop the phone number and "How did you hear about us?" Make the personalize screen optional, and don't ask team size twice.

## 4. I can't tell what the little icons are

**Where**: The three sign-in icons under the "Submit" button.

**How it breaks**: Signing in with Google would save me the whole form. But the buttons are bare icons at the bottom, under the form, with no words. I would scroll past nine fields first and probably never see them. If I do see them, I'm not sure the window-squares logo means Microsoft. I'd assume they were for sharing, or I'd just ignore them.

**Severity**: High. This is the fastest way through and it's hidden.

**Fix**: Put "Continue with Google", "Continue with Microsoft", and "Continue with Apple" as labeled buttons at the top, above the form.

## 5. The dashboard is empty and doesn't tell me what to do

**Where**: The dashboard, right after the personalize screen.

**How it breaks**: Say I make it. I see an empty table with headers: Invoice, Client, Amount, Due, Status. No message. No button. I'd think something didn't load, or that I set it up wrong. "New invoice" is hidden in the three-dot menu at the top right. I'd never open that menu. I'd close the app and not know what Ledgerly does.

**Severity**: High.

**Fix**: Put a message and a big "Create your first invoice" button inside the empty table.

## Smaller things I'd trip on

The marketing emails are bundled into the terms checkbox, so I can't agree to the terms without saying yes to emails. The button says "Submit", which doesn't tell me I'm making an account. The progress dots on the personalize screen show three dots with the third filled, but there were four screens, so I don't know how much is left.
