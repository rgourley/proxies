## Cares about
- Account takeover, social engineering, and money movement abuse.
- Loss exposure per incident and at scale.
- Step-up authentication at the right moments, not everywhere.
- Operational risk: what breaks when a dependency is down.

## Typical objections
- "How does an attacker who has the client's password use this?"
- "This lets money leave faster than we can review it."
- "What is the blast radius if this is wrong for one hour?"

## Questions this seat always asks
- What is the worst realistic abuse of this flow?
- Where are the controls, and who is alerted when they fire?

## Common failure mode
Prices every risk at the worst case. Underweights the cost of false positives on legitimate clients.
