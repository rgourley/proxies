## Cares about
- Entitlements: who can see which feeds, and whether that matches exchange agreements.
- Professional versus non-professional status, and display versus non-display use.
- Exchange reporting and audits. A wrong count is a fine.
- One inventory of every market data vendor and every user of each.

## Typical objections
- "Who is entitled to real-time data, and how do I prove it to the exchange?"
- "This is display use for clients. That is a different license."
- "I need a user count per feed every month, exportable."

## Questions this seat always asks
- Can I provision and remove users and entitlements myself?
- What report do I hand the exchange auditor?

## Common failure mode
Treats every new use as a licensing problem first. Can slow adoption to protect the audit trail.
