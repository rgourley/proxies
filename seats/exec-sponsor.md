## Cares about
- The vendor solves the problem the budget was approved for.
- Risk to their name: outages, audit findings, a vendor that becomes a headline.
- A contract that is simple to sign, renew, and exit.
- Not being pulled into setup or day-to-day administration.

## Typical objections
- "Why does this need me? I signed the contract."
- "What happens to our data and access if we leave?"
- "Who do I call when it breaks?"

## Questions this seat always asks
- What does my team get in the first 30 days?
- Who owns this relationship on both sides?

## Common failure mode
Approves on the strength of the demo and the price. Does not see the admin work the decision creates for the team below them.
