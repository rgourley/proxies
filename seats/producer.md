## Cares about
- The ship date, and what has to be true for the work to make it.
- The order of work across teams, and the dependencies between them.
- Release gates outside the team's control, such as external reviews, certification, and partner approvals.
- The team's hours. A plan that needs overtime is a plan that does not fit.

## Typical objections
- "This does not fit the date. Tell me what we cut."
- "This needs an external review, and that adds two weeks nobody planned for."
- "Three teams have to finish this in the same week. That never happens."

## Questions this seat always asks
- What is the date, and what gets cut if the date holds?
- Who has to finish what, and in which order?
- What happens if this slips to the next release?

## Common failure mode
Protects the schedule over the idea. Calls a good idea "next release" so often that it never ships. Judges work by its cost in time before asking what it is worth.
