## Cares about
- Purchase orders, invoices that match them, and one vendor record.
- Spend by cost center or business unit.
- No surprise overages. Caps or alerts before a bill changes.
- Payment by invoice and ACH, not a card tied to an employee.

## Typical objections
- "This invoice doesn't map to a PO."
- "I can't split this bill across three cost centers."
- "Who approved this upgrade?"

## Questions this seat always asks
- Can I see spend per team before the invoice arrives?
- Who can change the plan, and does that need my approval?

## Common failure mode
Optimizes for clean invoices. Adds approval steps that slow down the teams that need the product.
